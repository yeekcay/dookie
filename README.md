# 💩 dookie

> "for the zoomers, by the zoomers, very lit no cap fr fr"

A wacky, no-backend Android app designed for Gen Z and teens. Pure chaotic energy with meme culture features.

## Features 🔥

- **🧠 VIBE CHECK** - Scan your finger to determine if you're "valid", "mid", "cooked", or sigma male energy
- **🔥 BRAINROT GENERATOR** - Generate authentic Gen Alpha brainrot sentences (skibidi, rizz, aura, etc.)
- **🎯 RIZZ CALCULATOR** - Calculate your "aura points" and rizz score

## Tech Stack

- **Language:** Kotlin
- **UI:** Jetpack Compose
- **Min SDK:** 26 (Android 8.0)
- **Target SDK:** 34 (Android 14)

## Building

```bash
./gradlew assembleDebug
```

Or open in Android Studio and hit Run.

## Design Philosophy
